---
version: "0.16.0"
date: 2026-07-06
headline: "A big tradeskill release: plan the cheapest path to max skill, see your
odds on every combine, plus new Bandolier and Macro editors and a deeper
character stats view."
highlights:
  - "**Tradeskill Leveling** — a new page that builds a step-by-step plan to raise any tradeskill to your target, picking recipes by skill-up band and total cost. Choose \"fastest\" (fewest combines) or \"cheapest\" (least plat), and it factors in the cost of sub-combines you have to craft along the way, warning when a step pulls in a different tradeskill."
  - "**Combine success chances** — the recipe view now shows your success and failure percentage for each combine from your skill and crafting-stat gear, including the trivial breakpoint where you can no longer fail, plus a skill-up chance estimate. Jewelcraft/Alchemy/Poison Mastery AAs and Maelin's skill-up buff are applied automatically."
  - "**Tradeskills tab** — characters now show their trained tradeskill values, read from the Zeal 1.4.3 export."
  - "**Bandolier & Macro editors** — now available to everyone. Edit weapon-swap bandolier sets (guarded to gear you actually own and can equip) and your in-game social macros directly, including a new `/cancelbuff` builder that turns a buff search into ready-made cancel-by-name macro lines."
  - "**Tanking view** — the character Stats tab now splits avoidance vs. mitigation, models the mitigation softcap and melee hit chance, and folds in Combat Stability and Agility AAs."
  - "**Clearer stat bars** — base attributes now show their base value plus the green AA bonus separately, with a tooltip explaining the source."
  - "**EQ client check** — Settings now version-checks `eqw.dll` (EQW-TAKP) alongside the existing eqgame.dll / Zeal check."
fixes:
  - "An empty tradeskill leveling plan no longer black-screens the page."
  - "Custom trigger alerts now show the captured target in the alert text, not just triggers from imported packs."
  - "The regex editor accepts Go inline-flag patterns (e.g. `(?i)`) instead of marking them invalid."
  - "Buff stat bonuses now scale with your level, so buffed HP and similar stats match in-game values more closely."
  - "Food and drink stats are now applied to your equipped stats."
  - "Distinct items that share a name stay visible in search and gear upgrades instead of collapsing into one."
  - "Copied target stats no longer include crowd-control immunities."
  - "The spell-modifiers view returns empty instead of erroring when no Quarmy export is present."
---
