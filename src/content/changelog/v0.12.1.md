headline: "A fix for NPC level display: range-spawning NPCs now show their full level range."
fixes:
  - "NPCs that spawn within a level range (e.g. a Shissar Revenant at 50–54) previously displayed only their lowest possible level, which could mislead on level-gated mechanics like charm caps. They now show the full range (e.g. \"50-54\") everywhere a level appears: the NPC overlays (dashboard and pop-out), the Database Explorer NPC detail and list, the Zones NPC list, and global search."
